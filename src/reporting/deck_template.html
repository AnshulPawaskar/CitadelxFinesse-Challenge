1 · Problem and research design

Which 10 stocks to hold, and how reliable is the answer?

  • Universe: 300 NSE stocks (Nifty 100 + Midcap 100 + Smallcap 100), 2020 - 2026.
  • Rules: at most 10 stocks, Rs 1 crore capital, 0.1% cost per trade side, Rs 2 crore minimum daily turnover.
  • Method: test many ideas, fix the rules using validation data, then check them on data that was not used in the design.
  • Aim: a method with no look-ahead, with costs included, that others can repeat.

3 · Validation method

Design on one period. Test on another.

Training
2020 - 2021
Validation: design is fixed here
2022-01 to 2024-06
Holdout: rules fixed before testing
2024-07 to 2026-10
  • Walk-forward ML: the training window grows over time. Models are retrained every 63 days. A gap is kept between training labels and test data.
  • Rules fixed first: the best 3 signals and the rebalancing frequency are chosen by validation Sharpe ratio.
  • Extra features are used only if validation Sharpe ratio and drawdown both improve.
Why this matters: testing 100+ variants overstates in-sample results

4 · Signal research

Predictive power grows over weeks, not days

Rank IC by holding period (validation period)
Rank IC is the Spearman correlation between the signal and the later return. Validation period: 2022-01 to 2024-06.
  • Rank IC ≈ 0.005 at 1 day, ≈ 0.05 at 21 days: so there is no useful daily signal.
  • ML and DL models were not better than simple factors at 21 days.
  • So we combine momentum, MLP and risk-adjusted momentum to reduce reliance on one model.

5 · Rebalancing frequency

Quarterly: highest Sharpe ratio with one-twentieth of the turnover

Mean Sharpe across 13 signals, by rebalancing frequency
Labels show yearly one-way turnover as a multiple of portfolio value (validation period).
  • Daily trading has about 20 times the turnover and no better prediction.
  • Practical reasons: signals fade slowly, results are reported every quarter, mid and small caps trade thinly, and costs add up.
  • Plan: split the portfolio into three parts, one month apart, so the result does not depend on one date.

6 · Portfolio construction

Markowitz, risk parity and CAPM tested; equal weight kept

Sharpe ratio by weighting method (quarterly, 20% cap, Ledoit-Wolf covariance)
Rule: validation Sharpe ratio and drawdown must both improve.
  • No method passed the rule at quarterly frequency.
  • Estimation error is large with 10 stocks. Covariances are noisy and return forecasts are weak.
  • CAPM was the worst: beta does not explain mid and small cap returns. Minimum variance will be tested on future data.
