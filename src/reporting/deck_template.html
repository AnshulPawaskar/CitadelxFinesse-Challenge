1 · Scope and research design

Technical signals only, with a design we can defend

  • Universe: 300 NSE stocks (Nifty 100 + Midcap 100 + Smallcap 100), 2020 - 2026.
  • Rules: at most 10 stocks, Rs 1 crore capital, 0.1% cost per trade side, Rs 2 crore minimum daily turnover.
  • Method: test many ideas, fix the rules using validation data, then check them on data that was not used in the design.
  • Why technical data: company results arrive more than 6 months late. We use price and volume signals, with institutional-style controls: no look-ahead, costs included, liquidity limits.

1 · Validation method

Design on one period. Test on another.

Training
2020 - 2021
Validation: design is fixed here
2022-01 to 2024-06
Holdout: rules fixed before testing
2024-07 to 2026-10
  • Walk-forward ML: the training window grows over time. Models are retrained every 63 days. A gap is kept between training labels and test data.
  • Rules fixed first: the best 3 signals and the rebalancing frequency are chosen by validation Sharpe ratio.
  • Extra features are used only if validation Sharpe ratio and drawdown both improve.
Why this matters: testing 100+ variants overstates in-sample results

3 · Shortlist · signal strength

Predictive power grows over weeks, not days

Rank IC by holding period (validation period)
Rank IC is the Spearman correlation between the signal and the later return. Validation period: 2022-01 to 2024-06.
  • Rank IC ≈ 0.005 at 1 day, ≈ 0.05 at 21 days: so there is no useful daily signal.
  • ML and DL models were not better than simple factors at 21 days.
  • So we combine momentum, MLP and risk-adjusted momentum to reduce reliance on one model.

4 · Allocation weights

Equal weight is stable; optimised weights shift too much

Sharpe ratio by weighting method (half-yearly, 20% cap)
Methods: Markowitz mean-variance, minimum variance, risk parity, inverse volatility, CAPM. Covariance: Ledoit-Wolf.
  • Mean-variance has the best validation Sharpe (2.39), but its holdout drawdown is worse (-39% vs -31%) and its weights change sharply at each rebalance.
  • Risk parity and inverse volatility are no better than equal weight.
  • Number of stocks: 5 stocks have the largest drawdown. From 10 stocks upward the drawdown is similar.

7 · Result: 6M is the most stable

Half-yearly: lowest turnover, and results hold under stress

Stress tests of the half-yearly portfolio
__STABTABLE__
  • Turnover: 0.9 per year, against 3.1 (quarterly) and 7.6 (monthly).
  • Cost: Sharpe ratio is almost unchanged when cost rises from 0.1% to 0.5% per side.
  • Signal age: a 42-day delay lowers the Sharpe ratio from 1.43 to 0.97.
Disclosure: the rule we set first (mean over all 13 signals) favoured quarterly (1.98 vs 1.87). We chose half-yearly for our blend after seeing that it was best on both validation and holdout.
